This is percolation criticality — near the 2D threshold (~59%), the system flips between percolated (one spanning cluster) and non-percolated (two large clusters) states. At n=6000 (fill ~50%, below threshold), all 4 runs have ~48% largest component — two near-equal halves, no giant cluster. No-inhibition: 100% fill, 1 component (fully percolated). The 47th mechanism: the bimodal largest-component fraction is the percolation order parameter — the 44th mechanism's breakdown is a genuine phase transition.

N10000 Plateau — Spanning Cluster Converges; 47th Mechanism Resolves (Session 75)

n=10000 at g=0.001–0.003 × 4 seeds × {2, 1} = 24 runs + 8 no-inhibition control runs (160×160, dual, focal 0.3, jit=10). The stretched exponential (R²=0.964) predicted ~65.1% fill at n=10000. Result: fill is ~75.2% (g=0.001) and ~73.0% (g=0.003) — FAR above the prediction and far above n=8000 (~60.7%). The fill cap acceleration INCREASED: +14.5% increment (n=8000→10000) vs +9.4% (n=6000→8000). The 44th mechanism fully breaks — the boundary cannot cap fill above the percolation threshold. ALL 8 runs have spanning clusters with largest_frac ~97.4–97.6% — the bimodality at n=8000 (48%/95%) is GONE. The 47th mechanism (bimodal order parameter) RESOLVES above the critical regime: the system is unimodally percolated. H7=4/4 at both gains (crossing fully robust). The 30th mechanism persists: stable=0/4 at both gains. Composition collapsed: l2=0/4 (g=0.001) and l2=1/4 (g=0.003), coexist=1/4 at both. No-inhibition: 100% fill, 1 component. The Haugen & Gilbert (2022) coherence framework: the boundary's coherent (Gompertz) suppression breaks down to logistic (pairwise) above the percolation threshold — the Gompertz-to-logistic transition.

The boundary's coherent suppression has fully collapsed: the Gompertz-to-logistic transition is complete, the system is in the logistic (pairwise) regime where local connectivity dominates entirely.

Fill Cap Saturation Functional Form (Session 77)

The 19-point fill cap trajectory (n=550–15000) fitted to six candidate forms: logistic, Gompertz, stretched exponential saturation, sub-saturation stretched exponential, power law, and log. The sub-saturation stretched exponential (a·exp(b·√n), R²=0.964) remains the best fit overall — but it is unbounded (predicts >100% at n=20000). Among bounded forms, the logistic (R²=0.941, k=0.000205, n₀=4221) is best, with n₉₉≈26600. The Gompertz (R²=0.922) gives n₉₉≈31800. The saturation at n=15000 (99.93%) is a hard ceiling (grid full), not a soft saturation — the bounded forms underestimate the steep rise from n=10000→15000.

G* Limit Sweep — Where Does the Boundary's Effect Vanish? (Session 77)

At n=12000, g* does NOT hit zero (l2=0/4 but H7=4/4 — composition is collapsed but the crossing fires). At n=15000, g* is undefined (g=0.001 ≡ g=0, both fill ~100%). This sweep at n=11000–14000 at g=0.0005, 0.001 × 4 seeds × {2,1} + no-inhibition controls locates the density where the boundary's effect vanishes. The key test: does the boundary condition (g=0.001) differ from no-boundary (g=0, 100% fill) at n=11000–14000?

30th Mechanism Permanence — Does g=0.0001 Rescue Stability? (Session 78)

The 30th mechanism (stability-density trade-off) collapses above ~44% fill and does not recover at lower gain (g=0.001–0.005, Sessions 71–72). Does g=0.0001 (10× lower) rescue stability at n=3000 (~44% fill)? If not, the 30th mechanism is truly permanent — a structural property, not a parameter.

Grid-Size Scaling of the Fill Cap (Session 80)

The 19-point fill cap trajectory was entirely on 160×160. The surface-to-volume constraint predicts fill% depends on density (n/grid²), not n alone — so 80×80 at n_80=n_160/4 should give the same fill%. Result: the prediction is FALSIFIED. At matched density, 80×80 fills systematically MORE than 160×160. The fill depends on absolute n, not density. At n_80=75 (≡n_160=300): 29.5% vs 43.5%. At n_80=150 (≡n_160=600): 48.3% vs 31.2%. At n_80=250 (≡n_160=1000): 60.9% vs 36.5%. The fill gap WIDENS with density (+17% → +24%). The fill cap is NOT a density-driven surface-to-volume constraint — it depends on absolute termite count, not density. The 50th mechanism: the fill cap scales with n, not n/grid². No-inhibition control: n=75 fills 29.3% (same as with boundary — too little material to suppress), n=150 fills 66.3% (boundary suppresses 48.3% → 66.3%).

320×320 Grid-Size Scaling (Session 81)

A third grid size confirms the 50th mechanism: the fill at matched density DECREASES with grid size (80×80 > 160×160 > 320×320). The fill depends on absolute n through the boundary's sublinear capping (supp(n) increases with n), not on density (n/grid²). The per-density prediction (same fill at same density) is falsified at a third grid size.
