Over-Splitting → Under-Splitting Crossover

Session 98: r=16 on 160×160 (10% of grid) is STILL over-splitting — the degradation mode is fill-dependent, not r-dependent. The 30th ceiling is a dynamical phase transition. Queued-topics #254, #255, #256.

Finding: The 30th ceiling is FILL-DEPENDENT, not r-dependent. r=16 on 160×160 (10% of grid) is STILL over-splitting — the 80×80 r=8 under-splitting was a grid-size effect (the 50th mechanism). At low fill (~43–48%): over-splitting. At high fill (~64–76%): under-splitting. The fill fraction, not the r/grid ratio, determines the degradation mode. The 30th ceiling is a dynamical phase transition (growth rate ∝ n exceeds suppression rate ∝ √n), mapping onto Gelimson & Golestanian (2015, PRL 114, 028101).
