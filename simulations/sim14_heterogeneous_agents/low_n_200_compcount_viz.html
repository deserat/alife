Why the Count Is Not Universal

The 59th mechanism (Session 108) compared 160×160 (comp ~6.1, stable 0/4) with 200×200 (comp ~8.3, stable 0/4) and concluded the count was the critical variable. But on 200×200, both comp > 5-6 and r/grid < 5% were true simultaneously — two necessary conditions confounded.

This test separates them: at n=200-400 on 200×200, comp drops to ~5.2-5.6 (within the 160×160 threshold), but r/grid is still 4% (below the ~5% capping threshold). Stability is 0/4 and the boundary gap is ~0 at every n.

The count is a readout of the fragmentation dynamics at the fill where capping stops working — not the cause of the capping→no-capping transition. The cause is geometric: r/grid ≥ ~5%
