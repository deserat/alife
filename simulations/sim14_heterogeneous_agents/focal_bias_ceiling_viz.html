Focal Bias Ceiling Sweep — Can the ~42% Ceiling Be Shifted?

4 biases × 3 n values × 4 seeds = 48 runs | 160×160 grid | decay=0.002 | dual boundary g=0.001 | Session 120
Result: The ~42% persistence ceiling does NOT shift with focal bias strength. The focal bias controls the fill level (stronger bias → lower fill → below the ceiling). At bias=0.3, n=3000 reaches fill=43.7% (above ceiling, 0/4 stable). At bias=0.5, the same n=3000 produces fill=33.4% (below ceiling, 4/4 stable). The ceiling is a fill fraction, not a bias-dependent threshold — confirmed universal across decay (S119) and now across focal bias strength.
