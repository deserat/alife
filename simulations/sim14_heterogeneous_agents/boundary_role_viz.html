Boundary Role Test — What Does the Boundary DO, if Not Cap the Fill?

Session 114 · queued-topic #283 · 160×160 grid · 4 seeds · 2026-11-17

Key finding: At matched fill, the boundary is a marginal stabilizer at the phase boundary (~42% fill), not a composition mechanism. Below the ceiling (~35%), composition is perfect without it (cf=1.0, stable=4/4). Above the ceiling (~48%), both conditions fragment. The boundary slightly improves cf (+0.10) and stability (+1/4) only at the ceiling itself. The fill (deposit/decay equilibrium) determines composition quality, not the boundary.
