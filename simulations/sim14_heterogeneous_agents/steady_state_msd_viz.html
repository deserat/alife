Steady-State vs Growth-Phase MSD Exponents

Session 101 — Does the 30th ceiling exhibit G&G critical exponents at steady state?

Gelimson & Golestanian (2015, PRL 114, 028101) found α=1.72 at the dynamical critical point. Session 100 matched this in the growth phase (α=1.729). This test measures the steady-state exponent (6000 steps, late-window MSD).

Key finding: The steady-state αnc is subdiffusive (0.46–0.76) at all densities — NOT superdiffusive (1.72). The G&G match from Session 100 (α=1.729) was a growth-phase artifact.

The 30th ceiling is a dynamical phase transition (stability boundary) but does NOT exhibit G&G critical exponents at steady state. The G&G mapping is analogical (same balance structure: extensive growth vs intensive suppression) but not quantitative (different steady-state exponents). The growth-phase match was coincidental — the growth law produces superdiffusive dynamics that happen to have α≈1.7.
