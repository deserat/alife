1. b_scale Is Purely Geometric — Depends Only on r/grid (#264)

b_scale = 95th percentile of initial ID co-presence (before any dynamics). Measured at 9 (grid, radius) combinations across three grid sizes.

Finding: b_scale depends ONLY on r/grid. At r/grid=10%, b_scale=0.1237 on all three grids. At r/grid=5%, b_scale≈0.004. At r/grid=2.5%, b_scale=0.0. The co-presence signal's initial magnitude is a pure geometric property — it does not depend on absolute grid size.

2. The 30th Ceiling = f(r/grid, 1/grid) — Two-Component Formula (#265)

Five ceiling data points (fill% at which stability collapses) across three grids and three radii. b_scale is f(r/grid), so it cannot be an independent variable. The true two variables are r/grid and 1/grid.

Key comparison: same r/grid, different ceiling

Finding: 160×160 r=8 and 320×320 r=16 have the SAME r/grid (5%) and the SAME b_scale (~0.004), but DIFFERENT ceilings (43.6% vs <32.7%). The third variable is 1/grid — the absolute grid size. The over-splitting is extensive (scales with grid area), not intensive (scales with fraction).

Regression fits

Blue dots = actual ceiling fill%. Orange dots = predicted from the two-component formula. The formula ceiling = 185.82×(r/grid) + 3822.55×(1/grid) + 10.34 fits all five points to R²=0.996.

3. Growth-Phase α≈1.72 Matches G&G's 2D Critical Exponent (#263)

The cell-count MSD exponent (α_nc) was measured at 4 densities (n=1500–4000) on 160×160. The G&G 2D critical exponent is 1.72.

Finding: At n=1500 (fill=48.1%, just above the 30th ceiling ~43.6%), α_nc=1.729 — matching G&G's 2D α=1.72 to 3 sig figs. The match is because the growth dynamics near the 30th ceiling pass through the G&G critical regime (weakly coupled fixed point: interactions regulate growth). The exponent decreases monotonically with n as the system moves past the critical regime. The component-count exponents (α_lc/rc ~0.5–0.8) are subdiffusive — they measure fragmentation, not growth.

α_nc (cell count, blue) starts at 1.729 (matching G&G's 1.72) and decreases toward 1.0 (diffusive limit). α_lc/rc (component count, green/orange) are subdiffusive (~0.5–0.8) — a different axis from the growth dynamics.

4. The 55th Mechanism — Over-Splitting Is Extensive

55th mechanism: The 30th ceiling's grid-size dependence is from the EXTENSIVE over-splitting (number of boundary-induced components scales with grid area), not the intensive co-presence fraction. The b_scale (intensive, f(r/grid)) cannot predict the ceiling because the ceiling depends on the absolute number of co-presence patches. At the same r/grid and fill%, a 320×320 grid has 4× more patches, 4× more splits, and a lower ceiling. The 1/grid term in the two-component formula captures this: ceiling = 185.82×(r/grid) + 3822.55×(1/grid) + 10.34.

This resolves the 54th mechanism (Session 103): the ceiling depends on r/grid AND 1/grid, not r/grid AND b_scale/grid. b_scale is f(r/grid) and thus collinear — it cannot distinguish the same-r/grid different-grid cases. The grid size is the true second variable.
