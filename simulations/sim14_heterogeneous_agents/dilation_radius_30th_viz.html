30th Ceiling: Structure-to-Boundary Ratio

The 30th stability ceiling scales with dilation radius / grid size — not absolute structure size. Session 96, queued-topic #250.

Finding: The 30th ceiling is where the boundary's spatial granularity matches the structure's. A narrow boundary (2.5% of grid) over-splits the structure at ~40% fill. A wide boundary (10% of grid) under-splits it at ~64–76% fill. The crossover is the spatial analog of the KJMA diffusion-limited ↔ interface-controlled transition.
