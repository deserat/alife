# 2026-11-12 (Session 109) — The Critical Component Count Is Universal Across Dilation Radii

## The short version

The 59th mechanism (Session 108) identified ~5-6 components per half-region as the critical threshold for the capping→no-capping transition. The prediction: wider dilation tolerates more fragments because wider Moore-dilated footprints overlap more, maintaining connectivity. This session tested that prediction by varying r (4, 8, 10, 12, 16) on 160×160, measuring the component count at the 30th ceiling with 4 seeds each. The count is approximately constant (~4.6-6.1) across all radii — the connectivity prediction is FALSIFIED. The 60th mechanism: the critical count is a universal dynamical threshold (suppression rate vs fragmentation rate), not a geometric property of the dilation footprint connectivity. Wider dilation changes the fragmentation rate per unit fill (narrower boundary = more splits → reaches the count at lower fill) but the critical count itself (~5-6) is the same.

### Part 3 — The 60th mechanism: universal dynamical threshold

Component count at the 30th ceiling vs dilation radius (all 160×160, 4 seeds, g=0.001):

| r | r/grid % | n at ceiling | Fill % | Stable | Mean comp/region |
|---|----------|-------------|--------|--------|------------------|
| 4 | 2.5% | 2000 | 41.2 | 0/4 | 4.57 |
| 8 | 5.0% | 3000 | 43.5 | 0/4 | 6.12 |
| 10 | 6.25% | 3000 | 43.2 | 0/4 | 5.63 |
| 12 | 7.5% | 3000 | 43.3 | 0/4 | 6.13 |
| 16 | 10.0% | 3000 | 43.1 | 0/4 | 5.74 |

The count does NOT increase with r. It is approximately flat at ~5-6 across all radii (range 4.57-6.13, no systematic trend). The connectivity prediction (wider dilation → higher count threshold) is falsified.

**The 60th mechanism:** the critical component count is a universal dynamical threshold, not a geometric property of the dilation footprint connectivity. The boundary's coherent suppression can handle ~5-6 disconnected fragments per half-region, regardless of the dilation radius. Wider dilation changes the fragmentation rate per unit fill (r=4 reaches the count at 41.2% fill vs r=8-16 at ~43%) but the critical count itself (~5-6) is the same.

### Part 4 — Methodology lesson: buggy aggregation formula

The r=10, r=12, and r=16 comp values cited in Session 108's existing-data (comp = 2.6, 3.1, 2.9) were computed with a buggy formula: `np.mean(lcs + rcs) / 2` instead of `(np.mean(lcs) + np.mean(rcs)) / 2`. The buggy formula concatenates the lc and rc lists (8 values for 4 seeds) and divides by 2, producing a value half the correct mean. The corrected values (5.24, 5.90, 5.74) are consistent with the ~5-6 universal threshold. This is the same class of bug as the metric-ceiling rule (#61): always verify that your aggregation formula matches your intended computation by checking against a hand-computed example.
