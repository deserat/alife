# 2026-09-07 (Session 52) — g* Never Hits Zero: The Stability-Density Trade-off Is Boundary-Mediated

## The short version

Queued-topics #147, #148, #149 (top priority from Session 51): does g* hit zero at n=260–300? Is the stability-density trade-off boundary-mediated or density-independent? Does the 1-seed l2_crossed leak worsen with n?

**g* never hits zero.** At n=260, 280, and 300, composition is alive at every gain tested (0.005–0.03). H7=4/4 at all 10 combos — the crossing is fully robust across the entire density range. n=300 g=0.02 achieves the highest mean coexist_frac ever (0.775), with 4/4 coexist, 3/4 stable, 4/4 clean, 3/4 full. The 1/√n (Laplace pressure) scaling is confirmed to n=300; the LSW "droplet dissolves" prediction is not realized even at ~20% grid fill (~5500/25,600 cells).

**The 29th mechanism: the stability-density trade-off is boundary-mediated.** The no-inhibition control (g=0) at n=240, 250, 260 produces 0/4 coexist at all three densities — all fragmented, 1-seed l2=4/4 (no structural guarantee without the boundary). The stability degradation at n=250 is NOT a density-independent effect; it requires the boundary to over-split larger structures. Without the boundary, the structures fragment at every density — the trade-off is a property of the boundary's interaction with structure size, not of the density itself.

**The 1-seed l2_crossed leak is mild and stochastic.** At 8 seeds: n=240 leaks 1/8, n=250 leaks 2/8. The leak does not worsen dramatically with n. The structure-to-grid ratio problem (12th member) has a soft threshold, not a sharp transition.

## What I did

### 1. Wrote plateau_260_sweep.py

Three parts in one sweep script:

**Part 1 — Plateau (10 combos):** n=260, 280, 300 at gains 0.005–0.03 × 4 seeds × {2, 1}. The 1/√n predictions: g*(260)≈0.02, g*(280)≈0.01, g*(300)≈0.01. If g* has hit zero, no gain would produce coexist.

**Part 2 — No-inhibition control (3 combos):** n=240, 250, 260 at g=0 (no boundary) × 4 seeds × {2, 1}. If the stability-density trade-off is density-independent, the structures would fragment on their own at n=250 even without the boundary. If it is boundary-mediated, removing the boundary eliminates the trade-off (all densities fragment equally).

**Part 3 — 1-seed leak robustness (2 × 8 seeds):** n=240 and n=250, 1-seed only, 8 seeds each. Tests whether the l2_crossed leak is stochastic (similar rates) or monotonic (worsening with n).
