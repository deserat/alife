## What I learned

### The fill is density-dependent without the focal bias

At 23.4/kc with bias=0 (no focal, no boundary): fill = 66.25% (80×80) ≈ 66.49% (160×160) ≈ 64.26% (320×320). Spread = 2.23% — approximately the same across grid sizes. The curvature channel's local operations (1-cell Laplacian, 1-cell Moore dilation, 0.2-cell diffusion) produce the same fill at the same density regardless of grid size.

### The fill is n-dependent with the focal bias

At 23.4/kc with bias=0.3 (focal, no boundary): fill = 48.33% → 31.58% → 24.95%. Spread = 23.38% — an order of magnitude larger. The focal bias (1-cell steps toward a fixed home center) is less effective on larger grids: the same 1-cell step covers a smaller fraction of the distance to home on a 320×320 grid than on an 80×80 grid.

### The absolute-spatial-scale principle is confirmed

The 2.23% spread (without focal bias) vs 23.38% spread (with focal bias) shows the focal bias accounts for >90% of the n-dependence. The residual 2.23% may be 2-seed noise (320×320 had only 2 seeds) or a small residual from the 1-cell step size. The key distinction: LOCAL absolute scales (1-cell stencils acting on local shape) are scale-invariant at matched density; GLOBAL absolute scales (fixed home center, distance-dependent bias) are not.

### The 53rd mechanism's falsification is strengthened

Session 84 falsified the 53rd mechanism from one direction: the fill is n-dependent without the boundary (the boundary wasn't the cause). Session 85 confirms from the other direction: the fill IS density-dependent without the focal bias (the focal bias was the cause). The n-dependence was never about the boundary — it was always about the focal bias.

## Criticisms / limitations (honest)

- **The 2.23% spread is not zero.** The 320×320 result (64.26%) is 2.23% below the 160×160 (66.49%). This could be 2-seed noise (only 2 seeds for 320×320) or a small residual n-dependence from the 1-cell step size (which is also an absolute spatial scale, though a LOCAL one). The key point is the order-of-magnitude difference (2.23% vs 23.38%), not the exact spread.
- **Only 23.4/kc density tested.** The 46.9/kc density was not tested (320×320 n=4800 would take ~500-800s per run). The 23.4/kc results are sufficient to confirm the principle.
- **The 320×320 composition quality is poor (cf=0.000).** Without the focal bias, the agents spread evenly and the structures merge or fragment. The H7 crossing fires (2/2) but L2 composition is 2/2 (l2_crossed, not coexist). The fill measurement is valid even when composition quality is poor — the fill is about total material, not about coexistence.
- **The 50th mechanism at higher gains (g≥0.3) is untested (#235).** At g=0.01 the boundary is negligible (<0.5%). At higher gains the boundary may contribute to n-dependence, but the 50th mechanism was tested at g=0.01-0.02 where the focal bias dominates.
