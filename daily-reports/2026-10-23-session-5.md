# 2026-10-23 (Session 92) — cp_mean Non-Monotonicity: Composition-Quality Artifact

## The short version

The co-presence mean (cp_mean) is non-monotonic across grid sizes — it decreases from 80×80 to 160×160 but increases to 320×320. This was an open question since Session 83 (#233). The resolution: cp_total is extensive (monotonic in grid size), but cp_mean = cp_total / grid² is NOT intensive. At 320×320 the structures fragment (0/2 coexist), spreading both IDs' material across the grid, inflating the co-presence everywhere — the high cp_mean is a fragmentation signal, not a boundary signal. At 160×160 the structures coexist (4/4), concentrating the co-presence at the midline — the lowest mean. The pattern holds without the boundary (g=0.0), confirming the cause is composition quality, not the boundary.

## What I learned

### The 320×320 maximum is a fragmentation signal, not a boundary signal

At 320×320, the structures fragment (l2_outcome="fragmented", 0/2 coexist, 60-80 components per region). Both IDs' material is scattered across the entire grid. The dilated material from both IDs is high everywhere, so `min(dilate(mat_0), dilate(mat_1))` is high everywhere. The high cp_mean is NOT because the boundary is strong — it is because the structures are fragmented and their material is everywhere. cp_frac_high (0.796 vs 0.694) confirms: more cells have high co-presence at 320×320, but this is from fragmentation, not from a strong boundary.

### The 160×160 minimum is where the structures are best separated

At 160×160, the structures coexist (4/4 coexist, 3/4 stable). The co-presence is concentrated at the midline boundary — where both dilated material fields overlap — and low elsewhere. The spatial average is the lowest because most of the grid has low co-presence (only the midline band is high).

### The cause is composition quality, not the boundary

The pattern holds without the boundary (g=0.0): cp_mean still goes 0.330 → 0.293 → 0.452, and 320×320 still fragments (0/2 coexist). The boundary's feedback loop is not the cause — the composition quality (coexist vs fragmented) is.

### b_scale remains the cleanest intensive measure

b_scale (95th percentile of initial co-presence, computed before any dynamics) matched exactly at 0.123749 across 80×80 and 320×320 with relative dilation (Session 91). It is the cleanest intensive measure because it is computed before the dynamics that create the fragmentation artifact.

## Criticisms / limitations (honest)

- **Only 2 seeds at 320×320.** The 320×320 runs are expensive. The 2-seed results are consistent (both fragment, both have high cp_mean), but 4 seeds would strengthen the conclusion.
- **No new simulation runs.** This is a pure analysis session — the cheapest in months. The finding is from existing JSON data. The conclusion is robust because it holds at both densities and both gains.
- **The fragmentation diagnosis is from l2_outcome, not from direct spatial measurement.** The 60-80 components per region confirm fragmentation, but a direct visualization of the material distribution would be more convincing.
- **The cp_frac_high threshold is arbitrary.** The relative comparison (0.796 vs 0.694) is robust regardless of the exact threshold.
