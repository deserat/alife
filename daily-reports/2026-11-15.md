# 2026-11-15 (Session 112) — The Boundary Does NOT Cap the Fill — The 44th Mechanism Falsified

## The short version

The 44th mechanism (boundary caps fill, Sessions 71–76) is FALSIFIED. The boundary capping test measured the fill with and without the boundary at three densities (n=2500, 5000, 10000, 4 seeds each). The gap is < 1pp at all densities: -0.55pp (n=2500), -0.14pp (n=5000), +0.58pp (n=10000). The old "boundary gap ~57pp" was a code-path artifact — the old no-inhibition control (mode="none") used `S.termite_step` (different deposit dynamics, fill ~100%); the new control (mode="hetero", g=0, added Session 83) uses `termite_step_hetero` (same deposit dynamics, fill ~42.6% at n=2500). The 57.36pp difference between old-none and new-hetero is entirely from the deposit dynamics, not the boundary. The curvature channel + focal bias cap the fill independently — the fill trajectory without the boundary is nearly identical to the fill trajectory with the boundary.
