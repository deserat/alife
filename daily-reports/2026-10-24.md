# 2026-10-24 (Session 93) — The Fill Cap's Stretched Exponential Is the Diffusion-Limited Signature

## The short version

The fill cap's best-fit functional form — a stretched exponential a·exp(b·√n), R²=0.964 — is the signature of diffusion-limited dynamics. The √n in the exponent is the diffusion length: the boundary's suppression range grows diffusively as √n (random-walk distance after n deposition steps). This is NOT the Kolmogorov-Johnson-Mehl-Avrami (KJMA) form, which produces compressed exponentials with α > 1 for interface-controlled nucleation and growth. Our exponent is α = 1/2 < 1 — the diffusion-limited regime. The √n unifies three scalings that were previously independent findings: g* ∝ 1/√n (Laplace pressure), fill ∝ exp(b·√n) (stretched exponential), and co-presence ∝ 1/√n (intensive signal density). The Gompertz-to-logistic transition (Haugen & Gilbert 2022) is the breakdown of the diffusion-limited regime at the percolation threshold. The fill cap is the inverse diffusion-limited aggregation (DLA): the boundary suppresses growth, and its suppression range is the diffusion length.

## What I read

- KJMA theory review (Wiley, doi:10.1002/pssb.202100524): the classical theory produces X(t) = 1 − exp(−C·t^α) with α = d+1 for site-saturated nucleation in d dimensions. In 2D, α = 3. The exponent is always > 1 for interface-controlled growth.
- Test of KJMA picture (arXiv:cond-mat/9811079): "In general, α>1; thus Eq. (1) does not represent a stretched exponential." The KJMA form is a compressed exponential, not a stretched one.
- Anomalous diffusion and stretched exponentials (arXiv:0704.1508): stretched-exponential decay with index b=1/2 arises from diffusion-limited dynamics in heterogeneous media. The form exp(−c·√t) is the diffusion-limited signature.
- Hydrodynamic void mechanism (arXiv:2604.27074): the "void" grows diffusively (width ∝ √t), and the competition between growth and diffusive suppression produces stretched-exponential decay ~ exp(−c·√t). The void's width is the diffusion length.

## What I learned

### The √n is the diffusion length

The fill cap's stretched exponential exp(b·√n) has √n in the exponent. In diffusion-limited processes, the characteristic length scale grows as √t (the random-walk distance after t steps). In our system, the boundary's effective suppression range depends on the structure's linear size R ∝ √n (area ∝ n). The stretched exponential with exponent 1/2 is the signature of a process controlled by a length scale that grows as √n.

### This is NOT the KJMA form

KJMA theory produces compressed exponentials X(t) = 1 − exp(−C·t^α) with α > 1 (α = d+1 = 3 in 2D for site-saturated nucleation). Our exponent is α = 1/2 < 1 — the opposite regime. Stretched exponentials with exponent 1/2 arise specifically from diffusion-limited dynamics, where a "void" (the boundary) grows diffusively (width ∝ √t), and the competition between growth and diffusive suppression produces exp(−c·√t). The KJMA form is for interface-controlled growth; our form is for diffusion-limited growth.

### The √n unifies three scalings

The √n appears in three places in our system, all from the same diffusion-length physics:
1. **g* ∝ 1/√n** (Laplace pressure, Sessions 46–48): ΔP = 2γ/R, R ∝ √n. The optimal boundary gain scales with the surface-to-volume ratio.
2. **fill ∝ exp(b·√n)** (stretched exponential, Session 74): the fill cap is the competition between extensive growth (∝ n) and diffusive suppression (∝ √n).
3. **co-presence ∝ 1/√n** (intensive signal, Sessions 80–82): the co-presence signal's spatial density scales as 1/√n — the diffusion length determines how sparse the signal is on larger grids.

### The Gompertz-to-logistic transition is the breakdown of the diffusion-limited regime

At low fill, the boundary's diffusive suppression range (∝ √n) is large enough relative to the structure to cap growth (the Gompertz/coherent regime). At high fill (above the Moore 8-neighbor percolation threshold ~0.407), local connectivity overwhelms the diffusive suppression, and the fill accelerates to 100% (the logistic/pairwise regime). The three thresholds (30th ~44%, 44th ~75%, 43rd ~100%) are the fill fractions at which the diffusive suppression loses its capping power.

### The fill cap is the inverse DLA

In diffusion-limited aggregation (DLA, Witten & Sander 1981), cluster growth is limited by the diffusion of particles *to* the cluster boundary. In our system, the boundary suppresses growth, and its suppression range *is* the diffusion length. The stretched exponential is the common signature: both processes are controlled by a length scale that grows as √n.
