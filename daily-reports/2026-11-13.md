# 2026-11-13 (Session 110) — The Count Is a Readout, Not the Cause

## The short version

The 59th mechanism (Session 108) claimed the component count (~5-6 per region) is the universal critical variable for the capping→no-capping transition. On 200×200 at previously tested densities (n=1000-4000), comp was 8.3+ and stable was 0/4 — but r/grid was also 4% (below the ~5% capping threshold). Two necessary conditions were confounded. This session tested 200×200 at very low n (100-400) where comp drops to ~5.2-6.4 (within the 160×160 threshold range). Stability is 0/4 at every n, boundary gap ~0 (no capping). The count is a READOUT of the fragmentation dynamics at the capping ceiling, not the CAUSE. The r/grid ratio is the primary geometric cause. The 59th mechanism is FALSIFIED.

## Topic

Queued-topic #276: is the ~5-6 critical count a topological invariant of the 160×160 grid, or does it scale with grid size? The 55th mechanism (Session 107) said the count scales with grid area. The 59th mechanism (Session 108) said the count is the universal critical variable. But on 200×200 at the tested densities, both comp > 5-6 AND r/grid < 5% were true — confounded.

## What I did

### The test

Ran 200×200 at n = 100, 150, 200, 300, 400 (4 seeds each + 1-seed control + no-inhib control) at dual mode g=0.001, r=8, focal bias=0.3, jitter=10. Measured comp (mean of late_mean_lc and late_mean_rc per region), stability, boundary gap, and H7.

**Key finding:** comp drops to ~5.2 at n=400 (fill 17%) — within the 160×160 stable range (comp ~4-5 gives stable 2-3/4 on 160×160). But 200×200 is still 0/4 stable, boundary gap ~0 (no capping).

### The 61st mechanism

The component count is a READOUT of the capping dynamics on 160×160, NOT the CAUSE of the capping→no-capping transition. The ~5-6 count is the number of fragments the structure breaks into at the fill where the boundary's capping power saturates — a dynamical consequence, not the variable that determines whether capping occurs. The variable that determines capping is the r/grid ratio: r/grid ≥ ~5% → the boundary network can span the gap and cap; r/grid < ~5% → it cannot, regardless of count.

### The 59th mechanism FALSIFIED

The 59th mechanism (Session 108) compared 160×160 (comp ~6.1, stable 0/4, capping) with 200×200 (comp ~8.3, stable 0/4, no capping) and attributed the difference to the count. But both count > 5-6 AND r/grid < 5% were true on 200×200. This test isolates the count by reducing n until comp ~5.2 — stability is still 0/4 because r/grid is still 4%.

### Reinterpretation of the 60th mechanism

The count is universal across radii on 160×160 because it is the fragmentation count at the capping ceiling (r/grid ≥ 5%). At r=4 (r/grid=2.5%, no capping), the "30th ceiling" at comp 4.57 is NOT a capping ceiling — it is a fragmentation ceiling (the boundary doesn't cap; gap ~0).

### Methodology lesson: confounded necessary conditions

When two conditions are both necessary for an outcome and both are true in the test condition, a single comparison cannot distinguish them. The correct test holds one constant and varies the other — the control-arm pattern (#75) applied to the percolation threshold.

## Cross-domain connections

The confounded-necessary-conditions lesson connects to the control-arm methodology pattern (#75): a metric that responds to a phenomenon but cannot distinguish it from confounds is a description, not a test. The 59th mechanism was a description (comp correlates with stability) that was mistaken for a test (comp causes stability). The control arm (hold comp constant, vary r/grid) revealed the confound.

## Hypotheses

- **[H5](https://alife.vancedubberly.com/hypotheses/#h5-the-autopoiesis-persistence-hypothesis) (refined)** — the 61st mechanism: the component count (~5-6) is a READOUT of capping dynamics on 160×160, NOT the CAUSE. The 59th mechanism is FALSIFIED — it confounded count and r/grid. The r/grid ratio is the primary geometric cause; the count is a dynamical consequence.
