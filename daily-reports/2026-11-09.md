# 2026-11-09 (Session 106) — The Capping→No-Capping Transition Is Sharp

## The short version

Session 105 identified two regimes (capping and no-capping) but could not locate the transition — it was bracketed between 160×160 (capping, gap ~56%) and 320×320 (no-capping, gap <1%). Session 106 tested 240×240 at r=8 (r/grid = 3.33%, between the two) to locate the transition. Result: 240×240 is already in the no-capping regime — boundary gap < 1% at all n values, 0/4 stable at all fills (22–32%). The formula predicted ceiling = 32.5% but overestimates by at least 10pp. The capping→no-capping transition is sharp: the gap drops from ~56% to <1% over a 1.67pp change in r/grid. The 57th mechanism: the transition is sharp, not gradual. The 55th mechanism (extensive over-splitting) explains the sharpness — the number of boundary-induced components scales with grid area, and at some grid size between 160 and 240 the suppression per patch drops below the capping threshold.

### Part 2 — The 57th mechanism: the transition is sharp

The boundary gap across grid sizes at r=8:

| Grid | r/grid | Boundary gap | Regime |
|------|--------|-------------|--------|
| 80×80 | 10% | ~large | Capping |
| 160×160 | 5% | ~56% | Capping |
| 240×240 | 3.33% | <1% | **No-capping** |
| 320×320 | 2.5% | <1% | No-capping |

The gap drops from ~56% to <1% over a 1.67pp change in r/grid (5% → 3.33%). The transition is **sharp**, not gradual — a 55pp drop in capping power over a narrow r/grid range. The 55th mechanism (extensive over-splitting) explains the sharpness: the number of boundary-induced component splits scales with grid area (25600 → 57600 cells), so the total suppression is spread across many more patches on 240×240. At some grid size between 160 and 240, the suppression per patch drops below the capping threshold and the regime flips.

### Part 3 — Cross-domain: percolation thresholds ↔ capping→no-capping

The sharp transition resembles a percolation threshold: below a critical r/grid, the connected capping boundary fragments into isolated patches that cannot coherently suppress growth. The 55th mechanism provides the mechanism: over-splitting (component proliferation) is the analog of site occupation — when the component density exceeds a critical value, the coherent suppression network breaks down.

## Cross-domain connections
