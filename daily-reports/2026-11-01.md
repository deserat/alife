# 2026-11-01 (Session 101) — Steady-State MSD Falsifies the G&G Quantitative Match

## The short version

The steady-state MSD exponent α_nc is subdiffusive (0.46–0.76) at all densities — NOT superdiffusive (1.72). The G&G match from Session 100 (α_nc=1.729) was a growth-phase artifact. The 30th ceiling is a dynamical phase transition (stability boundary, correctly predicted by the growth-suppression balance) but does NOT exhibit G&G critical exponents at steady state. The G&G mapping is analogical (same balance structure: extensive growth ∝ n vs intensive suppression ∝ √n) but not quantitative (different steady-state exponents). The growth-phase match was coincidental — the growth law produces superdiffusive dynamics with α≈1.7 for unrelated reasons.

## What I did

### Steady-state MSD measurement

Ran `run_two_region_hetero` (same function as all plateau sweeps) with 6000 steps, sample_every=10 (600 samples), dual mode g_form=g_persist=0.001, focal bias=0.3, jitter=10, at 3 densities (n=1500, 2500, 3000). The detrended MSD was computed for three windows:
- **Growth phase**: first 3000 steps (samples 0–299)
- **Steady state**: last 3000 steps (samples 300–599)
- **Full**: all 600 samples

### Results

| 1500 | 48.7% | 37.9% | 1.543 | 0.986 | **0.755** | 0.983 |

G&G 2D critical exponent: α = 1.72 (superdiffusive).
Session 100 growth-phase α_nc (n=1500, 2000 steps): 1.729.

### The G&G match was a growth-phase artifact

The steady-state α_nc is subdiffusive (0.46–0.76) at all densities, far below G&G's 1.72. The growth-phase exponents in this run (1.316–1.543) are lower than Session 100's (1.255–1.729) because with 6000 steps the "growth phase" (first 3000 steps) includes equilibration time, reducing the pure-growth exponent. The 2000-step growth-phase exponent (1.729) was inflated by pure growth dynamics without equilibration.

### The fill equilibrates significantly

fill_early (48.7–64.3%) drops to fill_late (37.9–41.2%) — the system overshoots during growth and relaxes to the steady-state fill. The 30th ceiling (~43.6%) is the steady-state fill boundary, not the growth-phase fill.

### Component count exponents are zero at steady state

The per-region component counts (α_lc, α_rc) are constant (1 component per region) with zero variance at steady state. The fluctuations are in the total cell count, not in the number of fragments. The steady-state dynamics are bounded fluctuations of the total cell count, not fragmentation dynamics.

### The G&G mapping is analogical, not quantitative

The balance structure matches (extensive growth ∝ n vs intensive suppression ∝ √n), and the 30th ceiling location is correctly predicted by the balance formula (R(n) crossing 1.0 at n≈3000). But the steady-state critical exponent does NOT match G&G's α=1.72. The growth-phase match (1.729) was coincidental: the growth law (curvature channel concentrating deposits) produces superdiffusive growth dynamics that happen to have the same exponent as G&G's critical fluctuations.
