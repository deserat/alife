## What I learned

### The fill cap saturates at 100% — the boundary's capping power is zero, not just finite

Session 73 found the fill cap exceeds the percolation threshold at n=8000 (~61%). Session 75 found the fill cap fully breaks at n=10000 (~75%). Session 76 finds the fill cap saturates at ~100% at n=15000 — the boundary has zero capping power. The fill cap's trajectory from ~41% (n=1200) through ~75% (n=10000) to ~100% (n=15000) traces the complete breakdown of the boundary's coherent suppression.

### The 48th mechanism was a transient, not a property

Session 75 found the spanning cluster fraction converges to ~97.5% — the boundary retains ~25% as void even when percolated. Session 76 finds the fraction is 100% at n=15000 (ncomp=1) — the void is gone. The ~25% void existed only in the transition regime (n=10000), not in the fully percolated regime (n=15000). The 48th mechanism was the boundary's last gasp, not a stable property.

### The Gompertz-to-logistic transition is a complete phase diagram

The fill cap's 13-point trajectory traces four regimes: (1) coherent (n≤5000, ~38–48% fill, logarithmic fill cap, boundary dominates), (2) critical (n=6000–8000, ~51–61% fill, bimodal percolation order parameter), (3) transition (n=10000, ~75% fill, 25% void, boundary retains residual effect), (4) logistic (n≥12000, ~92–100% fill, boundary irrelevant). The Haugen & Gilbert (2022) framework is confirmed end-to-end.
