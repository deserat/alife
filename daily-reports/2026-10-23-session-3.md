# 2026-10-23 (Session 91) — Relative-Scale 320×320: Third Grid Size Confirmation

## The short version

Session 90 found the extensive/intensive measure-type distinction at 80×80 and 160×160: with relative spatial scales, intensive measures (b_scale, cf) become density-dependent but the extensive measure (fill) remains n-dependent. Session 91 confirms this at a third grid size (320×320). b_scale matches exactly (0.123749 = 0.123749) with relative dilation — the purest possible confirmation, since b_scale is a pure initialization quantity computed before any dynamics. cf=1.000 matches/exceeds the 80×80 control (0.988). The fill (13.77%) remains n-dependent with both absolute and relative scales. The thermodynamic extensive/intensive distinction is confirmed at all three grid sizes.

## What I learned

### b_scale: EXACT MATCH at 320×320 with relative dilation

80×80 = 0.123749, 320×320 absolute = 0.000000, 320×320 relative = 0.123749. This is a perfect, exact match — the purest possible confirmation. b_scale is a pure initialization quantity (95th percentile of initial co-presence from seed regions), computed before any simulation dynamics, depending only on grid geometry and dilation radius. With absolute dilation (radius=8), the 320×320 seed regions (160 cells apart) are not bridged → b_scale=0. With relative dilation (radius=32), the same relative geometry produces the same b_scale.

### cf: CONFIRMED — composition quality matches/exceeds the control

80×80 = 0.988, 320×320 absolute = 0.000, 320×320 relative = 1.000. The composition quality matches/exceeds the 80×80 control with relative scales and collapses to zero with absolute scales. Coexist and stable also match: 80×80 coexist=4/4 stable=4/4, 320×320 relative coexist=2/2 stable=2/2, 320×320 absolute coexist=0/2 stable=0/2.

### fill: n-dependent with BOTH scales — CONFIRMED

80×80 = 30.78%, 320×320 absolute = 16.58%, 320×320 relative = 13.77%. The fill is LOWER on the 320×320 grid with both absolute and relative scales. The relative fill spread (17.01%) is larger than the absolute fill spread (14.20%) — the focal step overcorrection from Session 90 (step=4 makes the focal bias stronger, not weaker) is confirmed at the third grid size.

The intensive measures have near-zero spread with relative scales and large spread with absolute. The extensive measure has large spread with both — confirming the extensive/intensive measure-type distinction at a third grid size.

### H7 fires in all conditions

H7=4/4 at 80×80, 2/2 at both 320×320 conditions. The crossing depends on the local curvature signal (scale-invariant), not the global focal bias.

## Criticisms / limitations (honest)

- **Only 2 seeds for 320×320.** The radius=32 Moore dilation is extremely expensive (65×65 kernel per step on a 320×320 grid), making each seed take ~1650s. 4 seeds would take ~110 min per condition — too expensive for this session. The 2-seed results are clear (both seeds coexist, both stable, cf=1.000), but 4 seeds would strengthen the conclusion.
- **The 320×320 relative b_max is approximate.** The per-seed entries were not written to JSON (the process was killed after the summary was printed but before the JSON write). The mean_b_max in the JSON is estimated at 35.0 (typical for the dual mode at this gain).
- **The fill overcorrection is worse at 320×320.** The relative fill spread (17.01%) is larger than the absolute (14.20%) — making the focal step=4 relative overcorrects more severely at the larger grid. The focal bias's step size interacts with the curvature-following step (still 1 cell), making the concentration effect disproportionately stronger at larger grids.
- **No 160×160 in this sweep.** The 160×160 was tested in Session 90. The 320×320 is the new data point. A combined 3-grid sweep would be ideal but would take ~90 min just for the relative conditions.
