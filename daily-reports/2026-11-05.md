# 2026-11-05 (Session 102) — The 30th Ceiling on 320×320 Is Below ~25% Fill

## The short version

The 30th stability ceiling on 320×320 is below ~25% fill. Stability is 0/4 at all n values tested (6000, 8000, 10000; fill 40–58%), even at fill=39.67% which is below the 160×160 ceiling of 43.6%. Combined with Session 81's finding of 0/4 composition at n=1200 (fill=25%), the 30th ceiling on 320×320 is below ~25% fill. The absolute-spatial-scale principle is confirmed at three grid sizes: the ceiling fill% scales monotonically with the boundary's dilation radius as a fraction of the grid (80×80 10% → ~70%, 160×160 5% → ~43.6%, 320×320 2.5% → below ~25%). The 54th mechanism: the 30th ceiling is grid-size-dependent. The boundary's fill-capping power is negligible on 320×320 (~1% gap vs ~56% on 160×160). H7 is 4/4 at all n values (crossing robust).

### Stability is 0/4 at all n values on 320×320

Even at fill=39.67% (n=6000), which is below the 160×160 ceiling of 43.6%, stability is 0/4. On 160×160, stability was 2/4 at fill=42.08% (n=2500). At the same or lower fill%, 320×320 is unstable while 160×160 is partially stable.

### The boundary's fill-capping power is negligible

The no-inhibition fill matches the with-boundary fill to within ~1% on 320×320. On 160×160, the boundary reduced fill from ~100% (no-inhib) to ~44% (with boundary) — a ~56% gap. On 320×320, the boundary's 8-cell dilation radius is too small a fraction of the grid (2.5%) to effectively suppress growth.

### The absolute-spatial-scale principle confirmed at three grid sizes

The 30th ceiling fill% scales monotonically with r/grid:
- 80×80 (r/grid = 10%): ceiling ~70% (gradual, under-splitting)
- 160×160 (r/grid = 5%): ceiling ~43.6% (sharp, over-splitting)
- 320×320 (r/grid = 2.5%): ceiling below ~25% (over-splitting, severe)

The 54th mechanism: the 30th ceiling is grid-size-dependent. The ceiling is NOT a universal fill fraction — it is a ratio of the boundary's spatial scale to the structure's linear size, both measured against the grid. As the boundary becomes a smaller fraction of the grid, its effective suppression weakens, and the ceiling drops to lower fill fractions.

### All runs over-splitting

The late_mean_lc/rc values are 9–50 (vs 1–3 on 160×160 at the ceiling). The 8-cell boundary fragments the 320×320 structures into 20–50 components per region at n=6000. The over-splitting is the same degradation mode as 160×160, but much more severe — the boundary is too small relative to the structure to create clean two-region composition.

### H7 is 4/4 at all n values

The crossing is fully robust to grid size. The 1-seed l2 control is True at all n values (structural guarantee lost — the boundary is too weak to confine a single structure on 320×320).

### Determinism

Verified: n=8000, seed=42, two identical 2000-step runs produce identical cell counts (50944).
