# 2026-11-18 (Session 115) — The Composition Mechanism Is the Focal Bias, Not the Curvature Channel

## The short version

The composition mechanism is the **focal bias** — the exogenous spatial reference that keeps two agent populations separated. A 2×2 factorial (curvature × focal_bias, 32 runs, no boundary, ~35% fill) found: with the focal bias ON, perfect composition (cf=1.0000, 4/4 stable, 4/4 coexist) occurs with or without the curvature channel. Without it, fill→100% and structures merge — the curvature channel alone fills the entire grid. The curvature channel adds fill (9%→35%) and enables H7 (0/4→4/4) but is NOT load-bearing for coexistence. H7 (the trace→actor crossing) is a curvature-channel epiphenomenon, not the composition mechanism — perfect composition occurs without H7 (focal only: H7=0/4, coexist=4/4). 40+ sessions reinterpreted.

## Topic

Queued-topic #286 — what IS the composition mechanism, if not the boundary? Session 114 showed the boundary is a marginal stabilizer, not a composition mechanism. At ~35% fill, composition is perfect without the boundary. Which component is load-bearing?

## What I tested

A 2×2 factorial at ~35% fill (n=2500, decay=0.004, 160×160, no boundary, inh_gain=0):

- **curvature ON**: curve_follow=0.6, deposit_prob_gain=0.85, excavate_prob_gain=0.60 (curvature-routed deposit/excavate)
- **curvature OFF**: curve_follow=0, deposit_prob_gain=0, excavate_prob_gain=0 (uniform deposit/excavate, no curvature following)
- **focal ON**: movement_bias=0.3 (agents step toward home center when not curvature-following)
- **focal OFF**: movement_bias=0 (agents always random walk unless curvature-following)

4 seeds × {2-seed, 1-seed} × 4 conditions = 32 runs.

## What this means

1. **The focal bias is THE load-bearing component for composition.** Without it (curv only): fill→100%, cf=0, all merge. With it (focal only): cf=1.0000, 4/4 stable, 4/4 coexist — perfect composition even without the curvature channel.

2. **The curvature channel is NOT load-bearing for coexistence.** It adds fill (9%→35%) and enables H7 (0/4→4/4) but does not affect coexistence (cf=1.0000 with or without it, as long as focal is ON).

3. **H7 (the trace→actor crossing) is NOT the composition mechanism.** H7=0/4 in the focal-only condition where composition is perfect (cf=1.0000, 4/4 stable). The crossing is a curvature-channel epiphenomenon — it detects curvature-driven self-maintenance, not coexistence.

4. **The fill cap is focal-bias-dependent.** Without the focal bias, fill→100% regardless of decay. The focal bias concentrates deposits; the decay equilibrates the concentrated deposits. Session 113's "deposit/decay equilibrium" was measured with the focal bias.

5. **Cross-domain: exogeneity as the composition mechanism.** The focal bias is the two-wire principle's eighth member (Session 37): the signal must be on a wire the system cannot reach. The curvature channel's signal is endogenous; the boundary's signal is endogenous. Only the focal bias is truly external. The composition mechanism is not self-organizing — it is externally imposed.

6. **40+ sessions reinterpreted.** The curvature channel was studied as the composition mechanism. But it is a morphology optimizer + H7 enabler. The actual composition mechanism (the focal bias) was present throughout but assumed to be a "concentration helper" (Session 34). It is the load-bearing component.
