### The r/grid scaling is FALSIFIED

At r/grid = 5% (matching 160×160 r=8), the ceiling on 320×320 r=16 is below 32.7% fill — at least 11pp below the predicted 43.6%. All 12 2-seed runs are unstable (fragmented), with 19–68 components per region (vs 5–7 on 160×160 at the ceiling).

### The boundary's fill-capping power is negligible at all n

The no-inhibition fill matches the with-boundary fill to within 0.6% at all three n values. On 160×160 r=8, the boundary reduced fill from ~100% (no-inhib) to ~44% (gap ~56%). On 320×320 r=16 (same r/grid), the boundary adds zero fill capping.

### Why r/grid is necessary but not sufficient

The boundary's effective suppression depends on TWO components:
1. **Spatial reach** (r/grid) — r=16 gives 5% of grid, matching 160×160.
2. **Signal strength** (b_scale) — the co-presence signal's spatial density is 4× sparser on 320×320.

The r/grid ratio captures (1) but not (2). The co-presence signal is intensive — it depends on the spatial arrangement of material, not just the dilation radius. Even with r=16 (2× the spatial reach), the signal it encounters is too sparse to generate sufficient boundary suppression. This is the 53rd mechanism (extensive/intensive distinction) applied to the 30th ceiling.
